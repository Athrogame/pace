Parent or guardian approval

Pace is a student planner. For an under-13 plan, Pace stores school and wake times, assignments, activities, work logs, and optional sleep, energy, and stress check-ins. It uses that information to make schedules and local coach messages.

Under-13 plans cannot use Google sign-in, Google Calendar or Tasks imports, or AI. They are not public. Render hosts Pace, and Supabase stores deployed plans. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

By approving, you allow this collection and use. You can ask to review or delete the child's plan, or revoke consent at any time, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You will receive a follow-up email to confirm this choice.
